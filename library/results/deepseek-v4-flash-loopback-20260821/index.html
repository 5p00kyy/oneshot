How to play

Rotate the wire tiles to join the power source to every lamp at the same time. A wire carries power only where its connectors face its neighbors. Dead ends and loops are fine — every lamp just has to be lit.

Controls

Click / tap a tile to rotate it. Keyboard: ←↑→↓ move cursor, Enter/Space rotate, U undo, R reset, Esc open/close this menu.
