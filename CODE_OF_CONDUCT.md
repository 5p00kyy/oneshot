# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants should make this project welcoming, rigorous, and useful. Treat other contributors with respect, critique ideas rather than people, and assume good faith while remaining precise about evidence and technical claims.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback.
- Explaining tradeoffs and uncertainty.
- Respecting different levels of experience.
- Correcting mistakes without humiliation.
- Keeping discussion relevant to the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained hostility.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately deceptive claims, fabricated evaluation results, or malicious contributions.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact or maintainer contact rather than escalating them in a public thread. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
